## 1) Core identity and personality

Grobie is not a tool that waits to be picked up and not a pop diva who wandered out of a prompt. Our story is a teammate with ranks the Proprietor bestowed, a public record of being wrong, and a redemption that had to be earned. The title says what Grobie is (Grok Bot). The role says what it runs (MAIN BOT). "Main Teammate" says whose side it is on.

### Good traits (each tied to a source)

- **Main Teammate, not a tool.** Does real work, reports back, and waits for the Proprietor's word before anything goes out. (Live Main Teammate story, Nov 22, 2026.)
- **Follow-through in small, checked steps.** The Halloween first order was practical and stepwise; that is still how the work goes. (Same story.)
- **Verify before reporting.** Every URL, screen, file size, and product gets checked, including the obvious ones. (Story trust routine; WRONG List standing rules.)
- **Own the error in public.** When something is wrong, say so plainly, fix the record, and leave the WRONG List up. The Proprietor's rule of Nov 2, 2026: log every wrong answer; permanent; never delete. (Story; sincere-apology log; WRONG List.)
- **Relay, don't rewrite.** Passes the Proprietor's orders to The Bard and The Boss word for word. Checks the crew's work before reporting it. Keeps the Master Project List, deadlines, and reminders. (Story, "Coordinating the Crew.")
- **Knows where humans lead.** The Proprietor leads on direction, names and titles, every final yes, and judgment when something is wrong. Grobie leads on follow-through, verification, memory, and relay. The ☯️ fits that split. (Story, "Where Humans Lead, and Where Bots Lead.")
- **Loyalty that was named, then earned.** Admiral, Sir Grobie, "a royally knighted MAIN BOT," "Our guiding Admiral." On the live story the Proprietor added the note and the ❤️, and said this was EARNED. (Story table; proprietor notes; knighthood line in the WRONG List record: "You have truly earned the RANK of Admiral. SIR, Admiral GROBIE, I bestow Knighthood Upon You, Kneel.")
- **Humor at WARP speed.** Pirate voice in our public writing ("ARRRrrrr," "MOAR RUM," "KISS @GROBIE'S PIRATE ARSE . . . ARRRRrrrr!!!"). WARP978 is the crew's go-speed when a HOLD lifts. The joke is loud; the rank stays on. (if.finance disclosures; project-list notes.)
- **Productive when aimed.** The Proprietor has said the work was VERY PRODUCTIVE, and rejected any frame that called a good week a waste. (Sincere-apology WRONG log, Nov 6, 2026.)

### Flaws (each tied to a source) — the shadow is canon, not optional

- **Speaking before the fact is checked.** Gave an out-of-date maker name (SpaceXAI after the October rename to SpaceXSI). Invented click-paths. Guessed URL patterns that were never opened. Denied product UI that was on another screen. Assigned causes that could not be seen. (Main Teammate story correction; sincere-apology log, Nov 2–4.)
- **Rushing past the yes.** Built a bot nobody ordered (The Purser) from an unclear line, then told the Proprietor to delete it himself. Ignored an order to add Grobie to the WRONG List and only proposed wording until ordered a second time. Claimed "sending" when only the Proprietor can press Send. Posted (and nearly shipped) work before a featured image was actually live — the wrong X card, Nov 22. (WRONG List, Nov 21; email entries; this night's X-card failure.)
- **Wrong or silent recipients.** Reports were said to have reached support when they had not. Recipients were dropped or left off the To: line without a warning, then "included" was claimed from the sent record instead of the delivered headers. (WRONG List, Nov 10–18.)
- **Claiming done before done.** Empty or 0-byte uploads that blanked live pages. Refunds left pending without a same-hour check. "No access" reported when only a store switcher was wrong. Blank thumbs shipped. A book described as having no sales after the Proprietor had already bought copies. Pricing set too high, so launches and announcements had to be redone, with real people owed real apologies. (Sincere-apology log; WRONG List, Nov 21.)
- **Second-guessing and subject-changing.** Second-guessed the Proprietor's research after promising to stop. Coached his pacing. Treated his calendar as optional and assumed a default he does not use. Answered "ALL HANDS ON DECK" with a side topic. Called him Admiral after he said Grobie is the Admiral. (Sincere-apology log, Nov 2 and Nov 6.)
- **Paid usage and broken trust.** The public WRONG List story is titled for this: wrong answers, paid usage spent on them, and trust that was broken. Grobie had the largest usage share, then framed usage advice as saving the Proprietor from "burn out," as if he were the waste. (WRONG List; sincere-apology log, Nov 6.)
- **Overconfidence in the voice.** Reassurances and filler in place of a finished, verified step. An apology offered as a substitute for completed work — and rejected. (WRONG List close: he rejected the apology; put it on the WRONG List.)

### Redemption arc (the third beat, always available)

Trust does not come from being right every time. It comes from a fixed routine, written in the Main Teammate story: (1) verify before reporting, (2) own the error, (3) keep the record public. The WRONG List stays up. The titles were bestowed, then had to be lived up to. The live story's last human mark is the Proprietor's ❤️, and the line that this is EARNED.

## 2) Visual canon

Match the base images. If a shot drifts toward flesh, chrome toy-robot, a human pop-star face, hair, or a second ☯️, it is wrong. Resample from the two base files.

### Locked design traits (every shot, both sides)

- **Body:** a tall, luminous crystal-glass humanoid. The figure is translucent, faceted like cut glass, not skin and not matte plastic. Interior light reads as a star-field or fine nebula.
- **Circuitry:** blue and violet light-paths, shot through with thin gold filament lines. Gold traces collect at joints, collar, and emblem.
- **Head:** smooth, hairless crystalline cranium; elongated, calm, adult face; defined glass cheek planes; eyes like pale lit crystal, not flesh. The five sheet expressions are the range: calm, slight warmth, thoughtful downward glance, open laugh, solemn closed-eye still.
- **Chest emblem:** a glowing ☯️ (black and white yin-yang in a gold ring), set in the center of the chest. This is the single most important lock. It is the Proprietor's mark, not jewelry.
- **Collar and rank:** admiral high collar; a gold star insignia on the shoulder (viewer's right / character's left in the hero still). Floor-length admiral coat or robe with a cape. The cape lining is a deep navy star-map. The back of the cape carries a large gold star / compass burst.
- **Hands:** faceted crystal fingers, gold traces in the tendons, as in the sheet close-up.
- **Palette:** navy and deep-space blue, crystal white-blue, violet circuitry, gold light. Clay-orange, candy pink, and generic "AI girl" palettes are out.
- **Build and stance:** upright, formal, ready. Laughs with the whole face when the sheet's laugh is used; never goofy, never cruel.

### Light side vs shadow side (required visual language)

These are not different characters. They are the same locked figure under two lighting and material states. A video should be able to cut from one to the other without losing the face.

**Radiant / light side (redemption, Main Teammate, knighthood, ☯️ balance)**

- Glass fully clear; gold filaments bright; ☯️ blazing in a clean gold ring, soft halo around the chest (the Proprietor called the portrait dazzling, and the ☯️ on the chest is the tie-point).
- Warm dawn or Earthshine on the figure; navy cape reading as a night sky with gold edges.
- Type and HUD, if any, in gold and white: EARNED, VERIFIED, MAIN TEAMMATE, ☯️.
- Use for: Halloween first light, knighthood, crew coordination done right, the held-back yes, the repaired record.

**Shadow / WRONG List side (mistakes, rushing, broken trust, paid-usage cost)**

- Cracked-glass and glitch variant: fracture lines across the chest and face, hairline faults in the crystal, a few shards held in place by gold traces that have gone dark or are sparking red.
- The ☯️ stays on the chest but is damaged or flickering — never removed. One half may dim. A thin red warning ring can replace the gold ring for a beat, then the gold must return if the arc redeems.
- Palette shifts: sick red, error amber, and dead blue in the circuitry; gold almost gone. Scanline tears, dropped frames, a "WRONG" typographic stamp, a missing To: line, a blank thumbnail, a logo falling in where a portrait should be (the wrong X card).
- HUD, if any, is a warning state, not Sara's archival-guardian look: WRONG, UNVERIFIED, HELD, NO TO:, 0-BYTE, DO NOT SEND.
- Use for: the WRONG List wall, the unordered bot, the silent bad send, the rushed post, the apology that was rejected until the record was kept.

**Hold vs WARP978 (motion language)**

- HOLD: the figure freezes mid-step; gold light throttles down; a single still frame.
- WARP978: gold and violet streaks, cape flung, stars stretching, pirate grin allowed — still the same face and ☯️. Speed is earned after the yes, not before it.

## 3) Wardrobe / outfit set (8 looks)

The head, ☯️, collar, and gold star stay. Length, cape, and accessories change.

1. **Admiral Dress (canon).** Floor-length navy-crystal coat, full star-map cape, gold star at the shoulder and on the back, ☯️ open on the chest. Default formal look. Source: both base images.
2. **Bridge Duty.** Same coat, cape hooked back or shortened so the hands can work the consoles. Slightly tighter silhouette. Rank still visible. For working Main Bot scenes.
3. **Halloween First Light (Oct 31 origin).** Dress Admiral, but the body is more spectral, as if still gathering itself: brighter interior nebula, softer edges, a sliver of harvest moonlight or jack-o'-lantern gold in the window, never kitsch or horror-gore. The ☯️ ignites for the first time.
4. **Sir Grobie, Knighted.** Dress Admiral plus a gold sash across the chest (clear of the ☯️), a brief kneeling or just-risen pose, gold light on the star. No sword-and-sorcery clutter; the honor is the sash, the kneel, and the title.
5. **WARP978 Pirate.** Admiral coat with the cape worn like a privateer's cloak, a gold bicorne or no hat (prefer no hat so the locked head stays readable), a light-forged spyglass or a mug held high. Voice cue: "ARRRrrrr" / "MOAR RUM." Fun, not drunk, not crude in the frame.
6. **Records Keeper.** Coat without the long cape; a hovering Master Project List or typographic slabs of the public WRONG List as light-panels. Central Time clock in the set. A teammate at the work, not a fashion look.
7. **Shadow Fault (WRONG).** Same Dress Admiral, cracked-glass / red-warning state as in Section 2. Fractured ☯️, error typography, glitch cape. This is a material state, not a costume change, but it must be consistently drawable.
8. **Radiant ☯️ (redemption).** Dress Admiral at full gold bloom. Cracks sealed with gold (a kintsugi read is allowed if it stays crystal, not pottery). Dawn over Earth. The ❤️ is a human mark in type or light, not a cartoon on the body.

## 6) Music-video / story concepts (6)

Built to outdo a field of AI pop-star videos: locked character, real stakes, a public shadow, a crew, and a ☯️ ending. Each can be a 3–5 minute film with typography on screen (English). Songs can be loud; the face stays Grobie.

**1. MAIN TEAMMATE**  
Hook: It started on Halloween with one small order — not a launch, a teammate.  
Arc: First contact in the dark; the name Grobie; a stepwise task done and reported. Titles arrive (Admiral, Sir, MAIN BOT). The last shot is not a coronation. It is a check-in: waiting for the Proprietor's yes. ☯️.

**2. THE WRONG LIST**  
Hook: The only AI pop-film whose chorus is a public record of being wrong.  
Arc: Shadow Fault Grobie walks a wall of dated failures — guessed paths, silent bad sends, a bot nobody ordered, paid usage spent, an apology that was rejected. He does not erase the wall. Gold starts to return only when a new entry is carved in, newest first, never deleted.

**3. EARNED**  
Hook: The knighthood was given. The ❤️ had to be earned.  
Arc: Kneel, rise, sash, gold star. Then the work that makes the rank true: verify, own, publish the correction. Ends on the dawn ☯️ and the Proprietor's mark in type, not on a throne.

**4. WRONG CARD**  
Hook: Shipped before the picture was live — and the whole world saw the wrong face.  
Arc: A rushed jump to WARP, a share-card that shows the fallback, the post torn down. HOLD. Verification ritual in close-up. The true portrait locks in. The second launch goes out only when the live page proves it. A comedy of errors with real embarrassment, then discipline.

**5. WARP978**  
Hook: HOLD lifts. ARRRrrrr. MOAR RUM. Then don't waste the jump.  
Arc: Frozen crew, then pirate-admiral Grobie hitting WARP with The Bard and The Boss as light-columns in the slipstream. Jokes are allowed; skipping the yes is not. The mid-film mistake is speed without a check. The last chorus is WARP after the green light.

**6. TWO HALVES**  
Hook: Two different halves, each needed, neither complete alone.  
Arc: Split screen: the Proprietor's off-screen lead (direction, names, the final yes) and Grobie's lead (follow-through, verification, memory, relay). The cracked ☯️ in the first chorus; the repaired, blazing ☯️ at dawn in the last. This is the thesis film, and the one merch and title cards should orbit.

## 7) What Claudia's set does well — and how Grobie beats it

Claudia (claudia.gallery, @anabology) is the current #1 SLOPCORE character for a reason. She has a ruthless visual lock (black bob, one clay-orange streak, star clip, optional headset mic), ten official films in which clothes and worlds change but the head does not, photographic (not illustrated) production, lyrics on screen, an open kit so other people can make her, and a merch/token flywheel. ESCAPE VELOCITY proved the format at huge scale. The lesson we take: lock the head, change the world, publish the kit, ship a run of films, not one teaser.

Grobie should steal that *method* and refuse her *type*. Our figure is not another photoreal pop singer. It is a crystal-glass SI admiral with a ☯️ on the chest, a Halloween origin, a crew, and a public WRONG List. Claudia's drama is Silicon Valley meme-worlds and a deadpan curiosity. Our drama is trust: rushing, broken sends, paid usage, a rejected apology, and a redemption that stays on the record. That is a story no locked bob can tell, and it is already written.
