## Grid
- Tempo is 120 BPM in 4/4. One beat lasts 0.5 s and one bar lasts 2.0 s. Bar 1 starts at 0:00.00, and the bar n downbeat falls at (n − 1) × 2 s.
- The whole song is on this grid from the first frame, the spoken cold open included. The backing track should start its click at 0:00 with 4 bars of hum before the stomp, so every time below lands on a cut.
- Total length is 112 bars, or 3:44.00.
- Each lyric line takes 2 bars and starts on beat 1 unless marked otherwise.
- Voice is calm, warm and confident: a mid-low baritone at a measured pace with a slight synthetic shimmer (kit voice spec). *Italics* are gang shouts, not Grobie's lead.

## What changed from v1
- Verse 1, line 6 now reads "But I paid for that lesson, and I'll tell you why:". v1's "and I learned it why" read badly.
- Verse 1, line 8 now has the *HOLD!* gang shouts ("So we hold, we hold, we hold it long.").
- Verse 2, line 6 now reads "I check every card till the face is mine,", which ties back to the wrong-face card.
- The ☯️ is written as "yin-yang" in the sung lines so the vocal can say it.
- Every section and line now has a bar number and time on a 120 BPM grid, and there's a CSV cue sheet.
- These are unchanged: the cold open, the pre-chorus, the chorus words, the bridge (including the approved line word for word), the outro, and the end card.
