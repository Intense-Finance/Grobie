## (a) Paste-ready prompt (60 words)

Bald sapphire-blue crystal-glass head, glowing soft-white eyes with gold iris ring, gold brow-and-temple circuits, slight closed-mouth smile. Glass neck and hands, warm-white glow, no bones. Navy double-breasted coat, gold buttons and piping, stand-up V-collar, 8-point gold star on each collar side, gold fringe epaulettes, navy cuffs, two gold bands and star, white-on-top yin-yang on upper-left chest, navy trousers and boots.

## (b) Locked badge placement

The outfit master is the lock for the coat, collar, and badges.

- Yin-yang: on the coat's upper-left chest (character's left, viewer's right), about one-fifth of chest width. Measured on the outfit master at about 86 px diameter versus a coat span of about 449 px at the emblem row (about 19%). White half on top, black half below, in a thin gold ring. Do not center it on the sternum, move it to the back, or change its size or orientation.
- Collar: a stand-up navy collar with a V at the throat. One small gold 8-point star on each side of the collar front. Do not use a tall split-tab collar.
- Epaulettes: gold fringe epaulettes on both shoulders (canon).
- Cuffs: navy cuffs with two gold bands and a gold 8-point star on the outer face of each cuff (canon).
- Coat: opaque navy, double-breasted, two vertical columns of gold buttons, gold piping along the front edges, pockets, and hem. No back star, no extra chest badges, no skeleton under the coat.

## 4K masters

JPEG locks in this folder have a PNG master with the suffix `-4k.png`. Square sources were scaled to 4096x4096, 16:9 sources to 3840x2160, 3:4 sources (outfit master and proportions master) to 3072x4096, and 4:3 sources (anchor pack stills) to 4096x3072.

Upscale method: interpolated resize, not an AI upscaler. `realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan` was tried and failed (`vkCreateInstance failed -9`; no usable Vulkan GPU/ICD on this box). The pip packages `realesrgan` / `basicsr` were not usable here (no PyTorch, PEP 668 blocks a system-wide pip install, and a full CPU torch install would have exceeded the install window). Masters were produced with Pillow LANCZOS plus a light unsharp mask (radius 1.2, percent 60, threshold 3).
