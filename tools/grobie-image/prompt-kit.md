## 3. DO
- Keep it clean and PG-13 or lower: comic, heroic, calm or dramatic, never sexual or gory.
- Less anime, more real: aim for a believable real-human presence or a believable real machine. Use film-like lighting, real materials and adult proportions.
- Keep his face calm and confident, with the slight closed smile at rest.
- If his mouth is open, the inside glows gold light.
- Keep his coat closed (double-breasted) unless the scene needs the glowing energy core. The core is pure light, with no bones.
- Keep every badge exactly as in the outfit master: yin-yang on the viewer's right chest, stars on the collar and cuffs, and fringe epaulettes.
- Leave room for crop: the gallery thumb is 800 px wide, so keep his head and chest readable at that size.
- **Whenever he grabs or holds something:** hands firmly gripping the object, fingers wrapped around it, visible contact. Name the object and say which hand(s) hold it, e.g. "both glass hands firmly gripping the anchor shank, fingers wrapped around it, visible contact". Closed hands, not open palms.
- After generating, run `grobie-image check IMG` (add `--subject` for busy scenes, and `--grip OBJECT` when he should be holding something) and fix anything it flags.

## 4. DON'T
- No outside brand names, logos, trademarks, team crests, real flags or readable text in frame.
- No real-person likeness, no celebrity faces.
- No anime or chibi style: no big eyes, no cel shading, no exaggerated cartoon proportions.
- No hair, beard, human skin, makeup or human teeth color. No peach or pink tongue.
- No red parts / no red ear module: no red eye, red ear module or red LEDs (the canon head has none; the anchor v1 image added a red module by the ear).
- No missed grabs: no hands missing the object, no hands near but not touching, no floating grip, no open spread fingers in front of the object (the anchor v1 miss).
- No exposed mechanical gear on the head.
- No skeleton, ribcage or bones.
- No moved, extra or resized badges. No back star. No tall split-tab collar.
- No weapons pointed at the viewer, no gore, no adult content.
