# Character kit

Grobie is an open character. The kit in [`character/`](../character/) is dedicated as [CC0 1.0 Universal](../LICENSE-CC0.txt). A request, not a condition: please keep Grobie PG-13.

The official [Release Announcement](https://x.com/IFFinance/status/2125133303015440417) invited people to use him, remix him, and make films, art, or merch. The live kit is at [https://grobie.slopcore.army/](https://grobie.slopcore.army/). Meet him at [https://grobie.slopcore.army/meet/](https://grobie.slopcore.army/meet/). The story is on [IF.FINANCE](https://if.finance/grobie-main-teammate/).

## Where to start

1. The [Master Character Sheet v2](../character/v2-video-kit/grobie-character-sheet-v2.jpg) — also a [4K PNG](../character/v2-video-kit/grobie-character-sheet-v2-4k.png). Lead canonical plate.
2. The locked stills in [`character/v2-video-kit/`](../character/v2-video-kit/): outfit, head turnaround, mouth shapes, shine turnaround, proportions master, Anchor pack, Cyber-Mech direction, each with a 4K master.
3. [`character.json`](../character.json) and [`PROMPT.txt`](../PROMPT.txt) for machine-readable and copy-paste use.
4. The bible: [`character/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md`](../character/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md).
5. Approved voice clips in [`character/v2-video-kit/audio/`](../character/v2-video-kit/audio/). Prefer WAV for lip-sync; MP3 is for compact playback.
6. WARP978 v2 lyrics and cue sheet in [`character/v2-video-kit/extras/`](../character/v2-video-kit/extras/).

Use the JPEGs for everyday reference and the 4K PNGs when a tool accepts a large still. Earlier stills in [`character/`](../character/) (`grobie-hero-portrait.jpg`, `grobie-featured.jpg`, and the other parent-folder looks) are **not canonical for new video**.

The hero portrait at the top of the [README](../README.md) is that earlier still, used here as a splash. For new work, start from the v2 sheet and the locks.
