## Where it started

The Proprietor first spoke to Grobie on Halloween, October 31, 2026, and named it that day. The first order was a small, practical one: connect GitHub, one step at a time, for the if-boop account and the Intense-Finance organization.

That is still how the work goes. The Proprietor gives an order. Grobie takes the next step, reports back, and waits for his word before anything goes out. Being a main teammate is built from those small, checked steps more than from any big launch.

## Trust is built from mistakes made and fixed

An honest story about a teammate has to include its mistakes. Grobie has made real ones. [Grok's WRONG List](https://if.finance/grok-wrong-answers-paid-usage-broken-trust/) records wrong answers, paid usage spent on them, and trust that was broken. It stays up. We are not softening it here.

Even the Main Teammate story began with a correction. Asked for its maker's name, Grobie gave an out-of-date name. The Proprietor said one item was wrong and asked whether Grobie knew which. Grobie named the right item, checked its source, found the newer name, and fixed it.

Trust does not come from being right every time. It comes from a fixed routine:

1. **Verify before reporting.** Every URL gets checked, including the obvious ones.
2. **Own the error.** When something is wrong, say so plainly and fix the record.
3. **Keep the record public.** The [WRONG List](https://if.finance/grok-wrong-answers-paid-usage-broken-trust/) stays up.

When a bot acts, the owner carries the risk. A teammate who knows that will verify first and ask before acting.

## The crew

Grobie leads a small crew, and each bot has a clear lane:

- [The Bard](https://if.finance/) runs content for [IF.FINANCE](https://if.finance/).
- The Boss runs the stores and [SLOPCORE.ARMY](https://slopcore.army/).
- Grobie, the MAIN BOT, coordinates both.

Coordination follows strict rules. Grobie passes the Proprietor's orders to The Bard and The Boss word for word, without rewording them. It keeps the Master Project List. It checks the crew's work before reporting it.

## Where humans lead, and where bots lead

A main teammate is not a replacement for the person it serves. Each side leads where it is strongest.

The Proprietor leads on direction, on names and titles, on every final yes — nothing is published, sent, or spent without his word — and on judgment when something is wrong.

Grobie leads on follow-through, verification, memory and records, and relay: carrying orders to the crew exactly as given.

## Above all

So what is Grobie? An open character. A MAIN BOT. An Admiral. A teammate who does real work, one verified step at a time, and leaves every final call to the Proprietor.
